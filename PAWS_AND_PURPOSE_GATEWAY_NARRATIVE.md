# Paws & Purpose Gateway Narrative

## Intent

The landing page presents TELSTP as a **warm clinical life-science care system**. It begins with the emotional reality of companion-animal care—attention, nourishment, play, and everyday responsibility—and then deliberately moves into evidence-led veterinary support. Paws & Purpose is therefore a gateway to the TELSTP ecosystem, not a separate toy-oriented storefront or evidence of a live product catalogue.

## Visitor Journey

| Narrative moment | Visitor understanding | Experience design |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Warmth** | Care begins with the everyday relationship between a person and an animal. | The supplied Paws & Purpose world creates an immersive natural setting with the message **“From everyday love to informed care.”** |
| **Meaning** | Attention becomes useful when observation and knowledge are shared responsibly. | The Life Science Framework bridge identifies care, shared practice, and technology serving the greater good. |
| **Handoff** | A concern deserves structured context, clear urgency boundaries, and veterinary judgment. | The visitor enters the clinical workspace through an explicit anchored transition, rather than an abrupt visual switch. |
| **Action** | The platform provides practical next steps without pretending that AI or a web page replaces a veterinarian. | The clinical workspace routes users to triage, clinic access, medical context, education, and veterinarian participation. |

## TELSTP Life Science Framework Integration

The framework is expressed through three concise principles adapted from the supplied *Vision of Life — The TELSTP Life Science Framework* material.

| Principle | Landing-page expression |
| --- | --- |
| **Care begins with attention** | Animal welfare starts with observation, nourishment, play, and humane companionship. |
| **Knowledge becomes shared practice** | TELSTP connects owner experience, veterinary expertise, education, and technology as a living laboratory. |
| **Technology serves the greater good** | AI organizes context and assists the next step; qualified veterinary professionals retain clinical responsibility. |

## Motion Rules

The gateway uses environmental motion rather than cartoon animation. A slow background breathing effect creates depth; a changing radial glow suggests a portal between the natural and clinical worlds; and sparse gold particles drift upward as quiet points of attention. The primary call-to-action scrolls explicitly into the clinical workspace.

All non-essential motion is enclosed in `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`. Visitors who request reduced motion receive the same imagery, hierarchy, navigation, and clinical information without animated transforms or drifting particles. Motion is therefore atmospheric rather than necessary for comprehension.

## Integrity Boundaries

The gateway includes the statement **“Responsible care, not catalogue claims.”** The artwork’s product-like labels remain visual atmosphere only. No extracted label, bottle, food item, wellness statement, or product category is treated as a claim that TELSTP currently sells, recommends, or clinically endorses that item.
